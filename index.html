A new era in research management

An ERA action empowering R&I.

As research and innovation grow more complex, skilled research managers are essential to turn European Research Area priorities into practice. This factsheet explains why research management matters, how it fits into the ERA, and what it has delivered so far — and what's coming next.

Why does research management matter?

The Commission's strategic priorities for 2024–2029 place research and innovation at the centre of the EU's economy, to secure its long-term competitiveness and strategic autonomy. Strengthening research management capacity helps connect actors and activities across a more resilient, effective R&I ecosystem.

Where research management can make the difference

See the RM Roadmap project deliverable "Research Management in Europe: a strategic opportunity for strengthening R&I systems — Overarching Roadmap", 31 Aug 2025.

How does the topic contribute to the European Research Area?

Anchored in Article 179 TFEU, the ERA aims to create a single market for research and innovation. Capacity-building for research management is covered by dedicated actions in each three-year ERA Policy Agenda, and is also embedded horizontally across ERA activities.

What

Legal & policy documents

  • Council Recommendation

    Pact for Research and Innovation

    Sets out the R&I priorities Member States want to work on.

  • Three-year work plan

    ERA Policy Agenda 2022–24 & 2025–27

    Translates the priorities into concrete outcomes and ERA activities, co-created with Member States, Horizon Europe associated countries and EU-level stakeholders.

  • 2022–24

    ERA action 17

    Enhance public research institutions' strategic capacity.

  • 2025–27

    Empowering R&I

    A new era in research management.

Who

Actors implementing the ERA activities

  • Owner

    Council of the EU

    Owner of the ERA activities.

  • Strategic advice

    European Research Area and Innovation Committee (ERAC)

    Provides strategic advice on ERA priorities and activities.

  • Implementation

    ERA Forum

    Implementation discussion between all Member States, Horizon Europe associated countries and EU-level stakeholders.

  • 2022–24

    Core group

    European Commission, Germany, Hungary, Aurora — plus national and organisation contacts of all committed parties.

  • 2025–27

    Core group grows

    EARMA and EU-Life joined the core group.
